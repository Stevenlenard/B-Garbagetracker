What the trail is

A computed route that drives every truck-passable street once and turns back at dead ends, starting at the Central (Purok 1) entrance. It is generated from the street network, so it is reference material for planning and analysis. It is not GPS data and it is not stored in the system as a trip or visit.

Which streets count

Truck-passable means OpenStreetMap main roads (trunk to tertiary, unclassified) and residential streets. Service lanes are shown separately because many are too narrow for a truck; confirm them on site. Footpaths, steps and tracks are walk only. The SLEX/STAR tollway is excluded.

Area covered

The outline is drawn around the system's own purok geofences (10 areas) with a margin of about 120 m. OpenStreetMap has no official Balintawak boundary, so a few streets of neighbouring barangays near the edge are included.

Recorded coverage

Green streets had at least one GPS fix within 25 m (accuracy 40 m or better) from the trips recorded in the system that are not archived. These are the researchers' walk, bicycle and vehicle test runs.
